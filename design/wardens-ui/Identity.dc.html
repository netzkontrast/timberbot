The Wardens
Machines stranded in a poisoned wasteland. They run on badwater, their industry kills the soil, and the only thing they value is Data. Their purpose: make the land livable for beavers again.
Logo: a hex nut around an aperture. Machine outside, watcher inside. Reads at 22 px in the chapter header and at 512 px as the faction avatar.
